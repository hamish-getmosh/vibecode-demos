# Psychology · 002 onboarding value stack

## Emotional arc

```text
Curious → checked in → relieved → hopeful → oriented → in control → committed
```

## Konvo · give before ask

- **Acts 1–2:** Give eligibility clarity and a personalised number before any selling.
- **Act 3:** Explain who Moshy is and why the programme may fit *this* person.
- **Act 4:** Ask for time commitment and RM5 only after value is established.

## Hormozi framing (light touch)

| Moment | Lever |
|--------|--------|
| Roadmap upfront | Dream outcome named early; low effort so far |
| BMI win | ↑ Perceived likelihood of success |
| Metabolic profile | ↑ Likelihood via personalisation |
| Stat + 300g | Dream outcome made tangible and human-scaled |
| Safe hands | Trust after they have something to protect |

## Two calculation moments

1. **Eligibility** (end of Act 1): BMI + health profile → relief.
2. **Projection** (Act 2): Metabolic inputs → 12-month estimate → hope.

Deliberate pacing: earned wins before mirror/trust/book.

## Tone

- Doctor-led medical programme, not a diet app.
- "May be eligible" / estimate disclaimers; doctor confirms suitability.
- MY: GLP-1 and **doctor** OK in copy; no brand drug names in funnel.

## What we avoid before Act 3

Trust logos before they have a result; doctor grid before they care; RM5 before consult value; USPs as adjectives instead of steps toward *their* number.
