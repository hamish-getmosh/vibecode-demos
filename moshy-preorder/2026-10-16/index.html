How your pre-order works

Reserve your treatment for $199, then talk through the details with a Moshy doctor.

1. Ready to switch, or want to check eligibility first?

Already receiving medical weight loss treatment and looking to switch to the new format? You can pre-order now and skip the online eligibility check. If you’d like to check your eligibility before pre-ordering, use our free, one-minute screener for immediate results.

2. Reserve your treatment

Over 10,000 people are on our waitlist. We expect high demand as we introduce the new format to new and existing customers. Your $199 pre-order guarantees your stock allocation and goes towards your first month of treatment.

3. Talk it through with your Moshy doctor

Your consultation is included after payment. Your Moshy doctor will explain the treatment, assess your clinical suitability and confirm the right treatment level and ongoing price for your personalised program before you decide whether to proceed.

4. Decide with confidence

If you choose not to proceed after your consultation, or you’re clinically unsuitable, we’ll process your full refund straightaway and release your reserved place to someone else.

5. Get ready for treatment to arrive

Shipping is expected to begin around 1 December 2026. Your first month begins when your treatment arrives. We’ll keep you updated, including if anything changes or is delayed. We look forward to welcoming you to Moshy.

Your pre-order reserves stock. Your Moshy doctor confirms clinical suitability before treatment begins.

Before you pre-order

What does my first payment cover?

Your $199 pre-order guarantees your stock allocation and goes towards your first month of treatment. Your Moshy doctor consultation is included.

Will my ongoing program cost $199/month?

Programs start from $199/month. Your ongoing price depends on your treatment level. Your Moshy doctor will confirm the price during your included consultation, before you decide whether to proceed.

When does my first month begin, and when do I pay again?

Your first month begins when your treatment arrives. Your next monthly payment is taken a few days before your treatment supply is due to run out.

Does pre-ordering guarantee a delivery date?

Shipping is expected to begin around 1 December 2026. Your pre-order reserves stock, but does not guarantee a delivery date. We’ll keep you updated about any delays.

Can I decide after my Moshy doctor consultation?

Yes. If you choose not to proceed after your consultation, or you’re clinically unsuitable, we’ll process your full refund straightaway.

Is there a Moshy option available now?

Yes. Moshy already offers another treatment method. This pre-order is for the program with the new format.
